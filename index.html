Buffalo care tips

Practical advice from our own experience, for fellow farmers.

🐃 Keeping buffaloes healthy
  • Keep the shed clean, dry and airy.
  • Always provide clean drinking water.
  • Feed a balanced mix of green fodder, dry fodder and mineral mixture.
  • In summer give shade and cool-water baths; buffaloes feel heat quickly.
  • Follow your vet's schedule for vaccines and deworming.
  • Wash the udder and your hands before milking.
🤰 Pregnancy-time precautions
  • Give a dry period of about 2 months before calving, with extra nutrition.
  • Avoid slippery floors, rough handling, long transport and stress.
  • Prepare a clean, dry, separate calving space.
  • Arrange regular vet check-ups.
  • Call the vet immediately if calving is difficult or delayed.
📈 Marketing advice for dairy farmers
  • Sell directly to households and build trust through purity.
  • Deliver at the same time every day.
  • Add value with curd, Kunda Perugu and ice cream.
  • Take advance bookings for weddings and functions.
  • Stay in touch with customers on WhatsApp.
  • Keep records of milk yield and costs.

General guidance only; please also consult your local vet.
